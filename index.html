About me

Bridging wet-lab biology and code

My story so far

I come from a background in molecular biotechnology — pipettes, gels, and the beautiful messiness of living systems. Today I'm channelling that biology into computation: writing code to read genomes, analyse sequencing data, and ultimately apply machine learning and AI to biological questions.

I believe the best way to learn is in the open. This site is my public roadmap and lab notebook — a place to track what I'm learning, share what I build, and hopefully help other beginners find their footing too.
