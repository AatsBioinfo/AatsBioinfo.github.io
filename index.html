Project

Do BulkRNABert embeddings know which tissue a cell line comes from?

BulkRNABert is a transformer model pretrained on tumour RNA-seq data. I ran it on 1,657 cancer cell lines from DepMap and asked whether its embeddings separate the 26 tissue lineages better than plain PCA on gene expression.

They do not. With a kNN classifier, the embeddings reach a balanced accuracy of 0.40 and PCA on the 2,000 most variable genes reaches 0.60. The gap depends strongly on the lineage: lymphoid and lung cell lines are classified almost equally well by both, whereas pancreas, kidney and pleura are lost in the embedding.
